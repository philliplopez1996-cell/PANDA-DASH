# Panda Dash

A portrait runner starring the PandaPals panda: jump the rocks, stay under the hanging bamboo, fly through the gates and collect coins.

- `index.html` is the whole game, with all art embedded. Open it in any browser.
- Hosted with GitHub Pages from the `main` branch, root folder.
- Scores, coins, skins, stage progress and settings are saved in each player's browser.

## Modes
- **Endless:** run as far as you can; a new zone every 1,500 points.
- **Stages:** six fixed courses with a finish line and up to three stars.
- **Daily:** the same course for everyone, all day.

## Controls
Tap, Space or Up to jump (tap again in the air for a double jump), hold to fly through flight sections, P to pause, M for music, H for home.

## Characters
Play as the panda or Panda Girl, chosen on the Profile screen.
